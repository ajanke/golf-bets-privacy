Golf Bets — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Golf Bets is a private app used by a small group of golfers to score rounds and settle friendly side bets. This page describes exactly what it handles. It is a statement of fact about the app, and it is corrected whenever the app changes.

What the app collects

  • Your account. An email address and a password, given when you create an account or sign in. The password is never stored anywhere in readable form: the server keeps only a bcrypt hash of it, and it is never stored on your phone.
  • GHIN credentials, for whoever connects a GHIN account. The password is encrypted with AES-256 before it is stored. The login name you typed and the session token GHIN issues are stored without encryption. None of the three is ever written to a log.
  • Round data. Player names, GHIN numbers, course handicaps, the course and date, hole-by-hole scores, and the side bets in play. What each player owes is worked out from the scores each time it is shown, and is not stored.

What is sent to GHIN

When a round uses GHIN handicaps, the server sends each player's GHIN number, along with the course and the date, to GHIN at api2.ghin.com, so it can fetch that player's course handicap. Searching for a golfer sends the name and state you type. This happens for every player in a round, not only for the person who connected the GHIN account.

GHIN's own login requires a token from a Google Firebase endpoint, so the server requests one. That request carries no information about you.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • A sign-in token, held in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your email address, the name and GHIN number of the player you claimed, and the join codes, course names and dates of your recent rounds.
  • Scores you entered while offline, held until the phone reconnects and sends them.

Signing out removes the sign-in token. It does not remove the rest. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Who can see a round

Anyone holding a round's join code can open that round and see the players in it, including their names and GHIN numbers. No account is needed for that. Treat a join code as you would the round itself.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No money moves through the app. It works out what players owe each other and shows it.
  • Your data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising. Apart from GHIN, described above, it is not sent anywhere outside this app's own server.

Where the data is held

The app's server runs on Render. The database is Neon, in the US West (Oregon) region.

Deleting your data

There is no delete button in the app yet. Email the address below and your account, the rounds you own, and the scores in them will be removed by hand. Deleting the app from your phone removes everything held on the phone immediately.
