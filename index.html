Golf Bets — Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Golf Bets is a private app used by a small group of golfers to score rounds and settle friendly side bets. This page describes exactly what it handles. It is a statement of fact about the app, and it is corrected whenever the app changes.

What the app collects

  • Your account. An email address and a password, given when you create an account or sign in. The password is sent to the app's server over an encrypted connection and is never stored on your phone.
  • GHIN credentials, organisers only. If you connect a GHIN account so the app can read course and handicap data, those credentials are sent to the server and stored encrypted. They are never written to logs.
  • Round data. Player names, GHIN numbers, handicap indexes, the course and date, hole-by-hole scores, and the resulting settlements.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • A sign-in token, held in the iOS Keychain. Signing out deletes it.
  • A small amount of app state, such as which rounds you opened recently.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No money moves through the app. It works out what players owe each other and shows it.
  • Your data is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside the app.

Where the data is held

The app's server runs on Render. The database is Neon, in the US West (Oregon) region.

Deleting your data

Ask the person who runs this app. Your account and its rounds will be removed.
